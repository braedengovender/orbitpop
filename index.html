what orbit can do

a quick tour of what's inside, then how to pull the export it needs.

3 orbital views

switch between who doesn't follow you back, who's mutual, and who you haven't followed back yet.

sort & filter

order by most recently or oldest followed, or by username, and narrow to accounts followed in the last 7, 30 or 90 days.

orbit-back rate & ratio

see what share of the accounts you follow actually follow back, and your follower-to-following ratio.

compare two exports

upload an older followers export to see exactly who's left your orbit, and who's newly joined since then.

never flag

permanently mute an account from a list - handy for accounts you know just won't reciprocate.

install as an app

add orbit pop to your home screen for one-tap access, no browser tab needed.

getting your export

eight steps to pull the ZIP this needs - only followers and following data required.

1

open instagram settings

go to settings and activity.

2

open accounts centre

choose your information and permissions.

3

start an export

select export your information, then create export.

4

export to device

pick this as your export destination.

5

narrow the selection

deselect everything except followers and following.

6

set the format

date range: all time. format: JSON. media quality: low.

7

wait for the export

instagram emails you once it's ready - it can take a while.

8

download and submit

drop the ZIP into the box above - no need to unzip it.
